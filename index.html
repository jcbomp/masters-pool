How Scoring Works

Each entrant picks 6 golfers: 3 from Tier 1, 1 from Tier 2, and 2 from Tier 3.

Total Score = combined finish positions of all 6 picks. Lowest score wins.

Missed cut penalty: golfers who miss the cut are scored as (number who made cut + 1).
